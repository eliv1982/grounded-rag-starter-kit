Example Co. Information Security Policy (synthetic sample text about a fictional company)

Passwords. Passwords for company systems must contain at least twelve characters and must not be reused across systems. Passwords are changed immediately if compromise is suspected.

Incident reporting. Any suspected security incident, such as a lost device or a suspicious email, must be reported to the security team within one working day. Employees should not attempt to investigate an incident on their own.

Data handling. Documents marked confidential may be shared only with colleagues who need them for their work and must not be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.
